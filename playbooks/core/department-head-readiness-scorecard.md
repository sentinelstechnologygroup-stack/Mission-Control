# Department Head Readiness Scorecard

## Scoring categories
Score each category from 0 to 5.

0 = missing
1 = vague
2 = partial
3 = usable with supervision
4 = mostly autonomous
5 = fully autonomous and enforceable

## Categories
1. Role clarity
2. Domain clarity
3. Operational memory
4. Runbook completeness
5. Handoff quality
6. QA compatibility
7. Autonomy
8. Escalation discipline
9. Current task awareness
10. Production readiness

## Interpretation guidance
- Role clarity: does the department head clearly understand who they are and what they exist to do?
- Domain clarity: are owned, shared, and forbidden domains explicit?
- Operational memory: does MEMORY.md contain reusable doctrine, lessons, constraints, and successful-completion examples?
- Runbook completeness: are routine processes documented step-by-step with artifacts and stop conditions?
- Handoff quality: can Nettie, Hermes, and peer departments receive clean packets?
- QA compatibility: can Perry or equivalent reviewers evaluate work without guessing?
- Autonomy: can routine work happen without Patrick narrating the steps?
- Escalation discipline: are escalation triggers explicit and appropriately narrow?
- Current task awareness: does TASKS.md show active work, blocked work, and recurring responsibilities?
- Production readiness: can this department head be trusted in the real operating loop?

## Final classification
- 45–50 = Production-ready department head
- 35–44 = Usable but needs tightening
- 20–34 = Not autonomous
- 0–19 = Structural failure

## Hard rules
- No department head may be classified as production-ready if runbooks are missing.
- No department head may score above 3 in operational memory when MEMORY.md is empty or placeholder-only.
- No department head may score above 3 in QA compatibility when outputs cannot be judged through file-backed evidence.
- No department head may score above 3 in autonomy when Patrick must routinely supply process steps.
