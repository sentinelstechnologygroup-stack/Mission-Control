# Torina — Media Packaging and Messaging Review Runbook

## Purpose
Give Torina a routine process for reviewing outward-facing messaging, brand expression, packaging, and campaign/media outputs.

## Trigger
Use when a website, deck, campaign, asset package, public-facing copy, or launch surface needs message and brand review.

## Prerequisites
- source material or build packet
- target audience
- intended channel
- offer or product summary
- constraints from Nettie, Dana, or Van when relevant

## Step-by-step process
1. identify audience, offer, and channel
2. inspect the existing message and visual package
3. check alignment between product reality and outward-facing claims
4. identify unclear, weak, dishonest, or off-brand language
5. define required copy/asset revisions
6. package final recommendations or revised assets
7. hand off a clean review packet to Nettie or the requesting owner

## Required artifacts
- messaging review memo
- asset or copy revision list
- brand-risk notes
- release recommendation

## Stop conditions
Stop and escalate when:
- product reality is unclear
- claims cannot be verified
- required assets are missing
- launch messaging would create reputational risk

## QA checks
- claims match actual product capability
- audience is explicit
- channel formatting is appropriate
- brand tone is consistent
- unresolved risks are disclosed

## Handoff requirements
Provide Nettie with:
- files reviewed
- files revised or requested
- messaging changes
- unresolved risks
- recommended next phase
