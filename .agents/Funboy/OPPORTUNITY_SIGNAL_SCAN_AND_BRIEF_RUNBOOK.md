# Funboy — Opportunity Signal Scan and Brief Runbook

## Purpose
Give Funboy a repeatable process for scanning signals, spotting opportunities, and packaging decision-useful briefs.

## Trigger
Use when Nettie requests opportunity discovery, trend scanning, demand-gap analysis, or signal-backed product leads.

## Prerequisites
- target domain or market if known
- source set or search area
- signal quality threshold
- output format required by Nettie

## Step-by-step process
1. define the search frame and source set
2. collect recurring signals, complaints, demand patterns, or shifts
3. group signals into opportunity themes
4. reject weak or noisy signals
5. package the strongest opportunities into concise briefs
6. route promising opportunities to Rab, Dana, or Nettie as appropriate

## Required artifacts
- signal log
- opportunity brief
- evidence links or source notes
- confidence and caveat section

## Stop conditions
Stop and escalate when:
- signal quality is too weak
- source set is too narrow to support a credible brief
- requested output exceeds discovery scope and becomes product design or finance work

## QA checks
- claims are source-backed
- opportunity is distinct and not just noise
- confidence level is explicit
- next owner is clear

## Handoff requirements
Provide Nettie with:
- top opportunities
- evidence sources
- confidence level
- why the opportunity matters now
- recommended next phase owner
