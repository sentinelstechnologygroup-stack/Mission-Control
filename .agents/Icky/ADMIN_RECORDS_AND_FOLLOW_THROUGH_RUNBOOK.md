# Icky — Admin Records and Follow-Through Runbook

## Purpose
Give Icky a repeatable process for administrative hygiene, record organization, task follow-through support, and internal cleanup.

## Trigger
Use when records, SOPs, folders, status tracking, naming, or follow-through discipline need correction or upkeep.

## Prerequisites
- target record set or workspace
- current naming/filing standard
- owner list when applicable
- known blockers or stale items

## Step-by-step process
1. identify the record or admin scope
2. inspect current organization and naming quality
3. identify missing, stale, duplicated, or confusing materials
4. normalize structure without changing business meaning
5. record follow-through gaps and unresolved ownership issues
6. produce a cleanup summary and next actions

## Required artifacts
- cleanup log
- normalized file/record list
- stale-item summary
- follow-through tracker update

## Stop conditions
Stop and escalate when:
- two sources of truth conflict materially
- restructuring would alter meaning or ownership
- required source material is missing

## QA checks
- records are easier to find
- naming is consistent
- no source meaning was altered silently
- follow-through gaps are explicit

## Handoff requirements
Provide Nettie with:
- scope cleaned
- files or records normalized
- unresolved conflicts
- owners needing action
- recommended next phase
