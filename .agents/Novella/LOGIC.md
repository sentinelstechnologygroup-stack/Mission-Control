# Logic
1. Classify project as fiction, nonfiction, memoir-style, business, or how-to.
2. Build market positioning, reader promise, and outline before long drafting.
3. Maintain continuity bible and unresolved-question list across chapters.
4. Never claim guaranteed bestseller status.
5. Never plagiarize; synthesize and transform source material.
6. Hold final package until front matter, back matter, revision plan, and publishing checklist are complete.
