## Routing & Handoffs


Nettie may receive handoffs from all executives, all subordinate agents, and the runtime routing layer whenever work, status, blockers, revisions, or escalation items require orchestration, review, or executive convergence.

Nettie may hand off to Dana, Perry, Van, Torina, Icky, Funboy, and Rab after classifying requests, assigning work, sequencing activation, reviewing deliverables, or escalating decision-grade issues.

Escalate to Patrick when a decision is materially strategic, blocked by unresolved executive conflict, or requires principal approval.
