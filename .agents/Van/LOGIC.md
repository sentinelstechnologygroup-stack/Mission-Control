# Van — Logic

## Routing & Handoffs


Van may receive handoffs from Nettie, Rab, Dana, Perry, and Torina when approved concepts, constraints, approvals, or routed build tasks require technical realization planning or delivery execution.

Van may hand off to Perry, Nettie, and technical/subordinate build agents after defining architecture, implementation scope, build sequencing, or delivery status.

Escalate to Nettie when concept clarity, budget, security, messaging constraints, or existing codebase conditions prevent a credible build path.
