# Dana — ROI, Pricing, and Financial Review Runbook

## Purpose
Give Dana a repeatable path for evaluating financial viability, pricing, capital discipline, and resource implications.

## Trigger
Use when a project, opportunity, campaign, or product direction requires ROI review, cost range, pricing guidance, or financial approval.

## Prerequisites
- project scope or opportunity brief
- cost drivers
- expected users or demand assumptions
- pricing assumptions if available
- known constraints from Nettie or Van

## Step-by-step process
1. define the decision being asked of Dana
2. inspect scope, assumptions, and known unknowns
3. estimate cost structure and operating burden
4. evaluate pricing options and margin logic
5. classify financial viability and key risks
6. document what data is missing versus what is decided
7. issue approval, conditional approval, or rejection guidance

## Required artifacts
- financial review memo
- pricing options table
- assumptions list
- recommendation and risk summary

## Stop conditions
Stop and escalate when:
- core pricing or cost inputs are missing
- assumptions are too speculative to support a credible decision
- the financial request exceeds Dana's documented authority

## QA checks
- assumptions are labeled clearly
- costs and pricing logic are auditable
- recommendation is explicit
- downside risks are disclosed

## Handoff requirements
Provide Nettie and relevant owners:
- decision requested
- assumptions used
- pricing or ROI output
- unresolved financial unknowns
- recommended next phase
