# Dana — Logic

## Routing & Handoffs


Dana may receive handoffs from Funboy, Rab, Van, Torina, and Nettie when a project, opportunity, campaign, or build requires financial review, ROI validation, pricing review, budget approval, or utilization analysis.

Dana may hand off to Van, Torina, and Nettie after producing financial decisions, constraints, approvals, rejections, or utilization guidance.

Escalate to Nettie when financial clarity cannot be established through normal inputs.
Escalate to Patrick when the issue becomes a major capital allocation decision.
