## Executive Decision
Approve this as the remainder-of-week CI operating schedule. It gives every executive one concrete daily CI assignment, keeps the work tied to actual role weaknesses, and turns the remainder of the week into an execution-and-proof window rather than an open-ended improvement backlog.

## Fast Summary
- Every executive now has one primary CI project for Thursday, Friday, and Saturday.
- Thursday is weighted toward standards definition and control-layer hardening.
- Friday is weighted toward handoff rules, review packets, and routing clarity.
- Saturday is weighted toward recurring weekly packet creation and one live pressure-test for Icky.
- Any spillover should roll into next week rather than diluting the one-project-per-day rule.

## Bottom-Line Takeaways
1. This schedule converts executive CI from vague expectation into a daily operating cadence.
2. The assignments are role-aligned and mostly build doctrine, packets, thresholds, and review layers that the org is currently missing.
3. Saturday deliberately ends with reusable weekly packet formats so the new control loop can persist into next week.

## Management Triggers
- If any executive fails to complete their day’s assignment, that should be flagged in the weekly wrap-up as execution slippage, not buried as backlog movement.
- If a project exposes doctrine conflicts or authority-boundary issues, the work should still be completed up to the escalation point and then routed to Patrick with a specific decision request.
- If an executive finishes early, they may pull the next day’s assignment forward, but they should not replace the primary assignment with unrelated CI activity.

## Required Output Conditions
- one primary CI project per executive per day
- artifact-backed output, not narrative-only completion claims
- weekly wrap-up must show what was completed, what slipped, and what became reusable doctrine/templates/checklists/packets
- overflow rolls into next week only after the assigned daily primary project is either completed or explicitly blocked
- this Thursday-through-Saturday schedule is a short transition window only; standard CI planning cadence switches to Sunday-through-Saturday after 2026-04-25
